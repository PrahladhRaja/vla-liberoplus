# Teammates' summaries

Each team member adds a summary here as `<your-name>.md`. Code, results and plans go in the shared folders; see
[CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

Suggested outline for a summary:
1. **What you worked on:** model, method, and why.
2. **What you ran:** runs, task sets, protocol card links (`results/<model>/<run>/PROTOCOL.md`).
3. **What you found:** headline numbers with their source files, and what they mean.
4. **Caveats and open questions.**
5. **Where the large files are:** checkpoints, videos and logs on Turing (paths), or Hugging Face links.

A summary of the work received so far is in [docs/findings/TEAMMATES.md](../docs/findings/TEAMMATES.md).
